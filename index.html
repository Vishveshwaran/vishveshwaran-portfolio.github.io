About Me

I design and build firmware for medical-grade wearable devices — from bare-metal sensor drivers to production BLE protocols.

With hands-on experience across nRF52/54, STM32, and ESP32 platforms, I specialize in integrating complex analog front-ends like the MAX30001 (ECG) and digital sensors like the LIS2DUX12 (accelerometer) into reliable, low-power embedded systems. My work spans the full stack — from schematic review and flex PCB bring-up to Zephyr RTOS threading, BLE GATT service optimization, and deploying TensorFlow Lite ML models directly on microcontrollers for real-time blood pressure estimation.

I've debugged NFC pin locks at the register level, streamed 7 BLE devices concurrently through a custom gateway, validated BP algorithms against patient monitors, and migrated firmware across three generations of Nordic SoCs. I thrive at the intersection of hardware debugging and firmware architecture.
